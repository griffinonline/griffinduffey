an ai security site, rebuilt and handed over

g360 technologies MOMENT · builder [check: exact role] 2026 g360technologies.com

the problem

g360 sells two ai security products, promptvault (guardrails on every ai interaction) and repo audit (an agent that checks code for vulnerabilities), plus services. the site had to make both feel concrete to enterprise buyers.

when i picked it up, the live site only existed on one laptop: it was deployed by hand and the repo was empty. the design in figma had also moved past what was live.

what i did

  • got the source somewhere safe. rebuilt the code from the live site into the repo, so anyone on the team could work on it.
  • built from figma. home, promptvault, repo audit and the services pages, with product views that show the guardrails at work instead of describing them.
  • worked through review rounds. before re-applying anything from a figma frame, i checked whether the team had already moved past it in review, so decisions didn't get undone.
  • forms that send properly. moved contact and newsletter forms off a mailto link to a real server-side send, switched off until g360 connected their own email account.
