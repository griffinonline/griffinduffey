a domain move and a launch day

tahoe labs MOMENT · builder + client contact 2026 tahoelabs.ai

the problem

tahoe builds large biological datasets for ai. they were launching tara, a plugin that lets the ai agents people already use query those datasets in plain language, and moving the whole site to a new domain at the same time.

the team needed to keep editing copy themselves, and the launch date was theirs to call, which meant it could move.

what i did

  • a staging site and a production site. every change went to staging first, then to production through a reviewed pull request, both deploying on their own.
  • the domain move. tahoebio.ai to tahoelabs.ai, with every old url redirecting to its new home.
  • the tara launch page. a page with the onboarding prompt and install commands, so people could go from reading about it to using it in one step.
  • launch held on staging. when the date moved, the new headline, launch page and contact section waited on staging while the live site stayed as it was, then all went live together.
  • content in their hands. copy and collections live in sanity, so edits go live without a deploy. the sanity project moved into tahoe's own account at handoff.

how it went

tara launched on the new domain on the day tahoe picked. after launch i added their analytics, fixed a build that broke on its own, and closed the project out a few days later with nothing left open.

working together

i was the person tahoe's team talked to day to day, in slack, and the one making the changes. when they asked to hold parts of the launch the night before, the answer was "sure" instead of a scramble, because staging already had it all.
