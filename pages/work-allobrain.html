a site a non-technical team can run

allobrain MOMENT · builder + handoff 2026 allobrain.com

the problem

allobrain makes contact center ai: agents that answer calls, live help for human agents, quality scoring, and the themes behind every conversation, in 180+ languages. after a repositioning, they needed a site that explained one platform instead of a list of features.

after launch, the site had to be theirs. their marketing team isn't technical, and they didn't want to depend on us for every change.

what i did

  • the build. product, industry and customer story pages on astro and sanity, in english and french.
  • motion that knows when to stop. the platform diagram assembles once as you scroll to it, then holds still. an earlier version looped ten animations forever; we cut it after seeing the options side by side.
  • a quieter visual system. i moved the branding past the original brand pack, with one rule that stuck: nothing on the site is ever set in all caps.
  • the handoff. the code moved into allobrain's own github and the content into their own sanity account. after that, new work (like a use cases collection with 11 entries) went over as a patch plus a plain-language prompt their own ai tool applies.

how it went

allobrain runs the site from their own repo now, and their team edits it with their own ai tools, without a developer in the loop.

working together

the copy was approved by their stakeholders, so i treated it as fixed and kept every later change to design and motion. that kept review rounds short and nobody had to re-approve anything.

when a choice was hard to describe, i built a few versions side by side so the team could just point at one. small asks after launch still come in, and they get a patch and a prompt, not a lecture.
