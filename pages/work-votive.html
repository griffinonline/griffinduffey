the back office behind a chai company

votive founder · designer + builder 2024–now drinkvotive.com

the problem

votive is a non-alcoholic beverage company i started and run on my own: chai concentrate and single-origin tea kombuchas, sold to 15+ cafes and shops across the southeast. no investors, no ops team. every order meant an invoice, a shipping label, a row in a spreadsheet, and a reminder to chase payment.

the off-the-shelf options (shopify plus a stack of wholesale apps) cost a monthly fee per piece and still didn't do net-terms invoicing the way cafes actually pay.

what i built

  • a storefront with no platform fee. astro on cloudflare, with stripe as the source of truth for money. card data never touches the site.
  • a wholesale portal. cafes log in, order by the case, and see live ups rates at checkout.
  • "bill me later." wholesale accounts can skip the card and get a real stripe invoice on net terms, emailed automatically.
  • labels that buy themselves. once an order is paid or invoiced, the ups label is purchased and linked in the order sheet.
  • an order sheet that keeps itself up to date. every order lands in google sheets, itemized, and flips from unpaid to paid when the money arrives.
  • books from bank csvs. a small command-line tool turns bank exports into a categorized, tax-ready ledger.
